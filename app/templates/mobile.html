About NETRADEEP
AI diagnostic system for diabetic retinopathy
Platform

NETRADEEP is an AI-powered clinical diagnostic tool for automated diabetic retinopathy grading from fundus photographs using MobileNetV2 with Grad-CAM explainability and CLAHE image enhancement.
